Serin Yoon

Hello! I recently completed my Master's degree at Visual Media Lab, KAIST, advised by Prof. Junyong Noh. I am currently a Visiting Researcher at DGP Lab, University of Toronto. My research interests lie in Computer Graphics and Computer Vision, particularly focusing on Facial Animation and Digital Avatars. I am passionate about deriving meaningful insights and results through the use of geometric data. I'm always happy to discuss research ideas and potential collaborations. Outside of research, I also like taking photos.
